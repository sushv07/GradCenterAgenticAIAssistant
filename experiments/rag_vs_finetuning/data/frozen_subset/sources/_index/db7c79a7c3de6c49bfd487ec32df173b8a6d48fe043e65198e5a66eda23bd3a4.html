Programs, Advisors, and Deadlines (Master's)

To view detailed information about a graduate program, please visit the University Catalog.

*International applicants must follow the deadlines outlined on the Center for International Education website.

** Indicates STEM-Designated Degree Programs

Applicants should review the CPaCE website to learn if their prospective program is a self-support program. These programs have independent fee structures.

PLEASE NOTE:

For Credentials Programs: At the discretion of the master’s program, up to 12 post-baccalaureate credential semester units from an accredited institution may be applied to a master’s degree program in education. 

For Certificate Programs: At the discretion of the master’s program, up to 15 academic certificate units can be applied to a master’s degree program.
