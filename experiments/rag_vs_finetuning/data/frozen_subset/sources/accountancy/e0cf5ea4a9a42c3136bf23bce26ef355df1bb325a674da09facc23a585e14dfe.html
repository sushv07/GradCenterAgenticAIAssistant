Program Overview

The Master of Science in Accountancy (MSA) provides the additional 30 units required to complete the 150 semester units required for CPA licensure** in California.

  • Provides advanced knowledge in tax, corporate governance, ethics, and other topics important for accountants in government and industry.
  • Prepares graduates to deal with increased complexity in accounting practices, tax laws, and business methods fueled by new technology.
  • MSA graduates have career opportunities with public accounting firms, corporate entities, financial firms, audit & tax groups, and government/tax authorities.

**Accreditation & State Authorization

Features

  • Cohort-based model consisting of 30 units
  • 11-month program beginning both spring and fall
  • Classes on Monday - Thursdays evenings, and Saturdays
  • Access to an extensive CSULB alumni network
  • CPA Test Prep is integrated into the program
  • Career opportunities with corporate entities, financial firms, audit & tax groups, and government/tax authorities
  • Career advising and other workshops are available

Fast Track for College of Business Undergraduates

CSULB College of Business undergraduates can apply for the Fast Track program before graduation. Learn more about the advantages of entering our Fast Track Application.
